🎯 Top Revision Tips

💧 Exam Topics First

Focus on Water Worries, Climate Change, and Energy — they're your entire exam.

🔁 Space Your Revision

Study a subtopic, leave it 2 days, return to it. Spaced repetition improves long-term memory.

🃏 Flashcards First

Learn key terms with flashcards before challenging yourself with quizzes.

📝 Case Studies

Name real places: Boscastle/Bangladesh (flooding), Alberta Tar Sands, Paris Agreement, Hornsea/Dinorwig.

⚡ Mixed Quiz

Once confident, tackle the mixed quiz — closest to real exam conditions.
