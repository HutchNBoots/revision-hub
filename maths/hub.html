💡 Maths Revision Tips

🖊

Always Show Your Working

Even if you get the wrong answer, you can earn method marks. Write down every step clearly.

🔎

Check With the Inverse

After solving an equation, substitute your answer back in to verify it works. For + check with −, for × check with ÷.

📈

Use Estimation First

Before calculating, round to 1 significant figure to estimate the answer. This catches big mistakes.

🅿

Draw a Diagram

For geometry, bearings, and word problems, always sketch the situation. A good diagram is worth marks.

📒

Read the Question Twice

Underline what you're asked to find. Many marks are lost by answering a different question.

🔧

Learn Your Formula Triangles

Speed÷Distance÷Time, Mass÷Density÷Volume, Force÷Pressure÷Area — cover the one you want to find.

📹

Units Matter!

Always check your units. Convert to the same unit before calculating. Area = cm², Volume = cm³.

🌟

Practise Weak Topics Daily

Use the flashcard “Study Not Yet” mode to focus on cards you haven't mastered yet.
