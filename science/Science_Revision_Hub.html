📋 Command Word Guide
State / Name
Write a simple fact or statement — no explanation needed.
Describe
Say what something is like or give an account of what happens.
Explain
Give a reason WHY. Use the word "because" to link cause and effect.
Compare
Point out similarities AND differences between two things.
Calculate
Work out a numerical answer. Always show your working!
Evaluate
Give good points AND bad points, then make a justified overall judgement.
Suggest
Use your scientific knowledge to propose an idea — there may be more than one correct answer.
Write a word equation
Reactants → Products. Use an arrow, not an equals sign.
Revision Tips
🧠
Space your revision out
Do a little every day rather than cramming. Revisiting a topic after a day or two moves it from short-term to long-term memory.
⏱️
Work in 20–40 minute chunks
Your brain works best in short bursts. Take a 5–10 minute break between sessions — get up and move around.
📋
Use the "Not Yet" deck
After going through a flashcard deck, use the "Study Not Yet" button to drill only the cards you struggled with. Focused repetition beats re-reading everything.
✏️
Write it out, don't just read it
After each topic, close your notes and try to write down everything you remember. This is called the retrieval practice effect — it's one of the most powerful revision techniques.
🔄
Generate more questions when ready
Once you've mastered the 20 seed questions in a quiz, hit "Generate 5 New Questions" and pick a subtopic you find tricky. Fresh questions stop you just memorising the answers.
